01About us

A house with a stove in every room

Right in the centre of Lviv there is a café-museum full of antique tiled stoves — “piets” in the local dialect. Each one has a little plaque telling you where it came from.

Between the stoves you’ll find old furniture and wooden radiograms, with gentle vintage music playing in the rooms. People drop in to warm up — and stay longer than they planned.

The kitchen serves Galician home-style dishes, house liqueurs and desserts. And the coffee is brewed right on the stove.
